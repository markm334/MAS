Multifunctional Assistive System (MAS)

The Multifunctional Assistive System (MAS) is a groundbreaking solution designed to enhance the quality of life for individuals with disabilities. Developed with a strong focus on sustainability and user empowerment, MAS combines advanced assistive technologies with eco-friendly materials such as bamboo fibers, corn-starch-based bioplastics, and recycled aluminum. It features a modular, lightweight, and foldable design, making it easy to transport and use in both urban and rural settings. Integrated smart sensors, kinetic energy harvesting, and long-lasting battery systems ensure reliable performance, while its intuitive interface offers seamless interaction for users. MAS not only supports mobility but also promotes independence, inclusion, and environmental responsibility.

Smart Navigation

Smart Navigation is at the heart of MAS, providing users with advanced integrated sensors that detect obstacles and hazards in real time. This technology ensures safer movement both indoors and outdoors, giving users confidence and independence. The system adapts to different environments, automatically adjusting sensitivity and alerting users to potential dangers, making navigation effortless and secure.

  • Real-time obstacle detection
  • Adaptive sensor technology for various terrains
  • Audible and visual alerts for enhanced safety
  • Supports independent mobility for all users
Energy Harvesting

MAS utilizes innovative energy harvesting systems, including kinetic and solar technologies, to power the device. This reduces the need for frequent external charging and ensures continuous operation. The kinetic system captures energy from user movement, while integrated solar panels provide supplemental power, making MAS highly efficient and environmentally friendly.

  • Kinetic energy harvesting from user motion
  • Solar panels for sustainable power
  • Extended battery life for longer use
  • Minimizes reliance on external charging
